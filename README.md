## 🧠 About Me

I'm Sri Sasank, a third-year Electronics and Communication student specializing in VLSI at **VIT-AP**, based in **Nallajerla, India**.

I'm drawn to the challenge of understanding how transistor-level behavior and physical implementation decisions shape the performance of digital circuits. Physical Design brings that curiosity together with timing, layout, and practical engineering constraints. India's growing semiconductor ecosystem further motivates me to build the skills to contribute to this field.

Through university coursework and independent practice, I'm developing my understanding of the **RTL-to-GDSII flow**, with a particular interest in **Clock Tree Synthesis (CTS)** and **Static Timing Analysis (STA)**.
